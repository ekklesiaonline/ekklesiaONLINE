# Clergy ordination, office, and authorization plan

**Status:** Proposed design for review; no application code or data has been changed.  
**Basis:** `pasted_content.txt`, current ekklesiaRD repository, and the Episcopal sources below.  
**Recommendation:** Keep administrative rank, ordained order, ecclesial office, and application permission **separate**, linked by time-bounded and entity-scoped assignments. Reuse `leadership_position` rather than introducing a second generic role-assignment table.

**First-release policy decisions:** Section 5 answers the four previously open questions with recommended implementation defaults. They support any configured diocese in The Episcopal Church, without claiming that every diocese has adopted identical local canonical policy. The relevant diocesan authority should confirm local-canon exceptions before activating consequential approval workflows.

## 1. Reconcile the proposal with this codebase

The attachment's separation of identity and authorization is right, but three details need correction:

1. **The 4–0 number is not a geographic access grant.** `app/Controllers/AdminHierarchyGuard.php:17-56` calculates the *highest Shield admin-group priority of the actor and target user* and prevents mutation of a higher-priority **account**. It does not decide which diocese/parish an actor may access. Preserve it as an account-protection guard; do not rename `4` to “TEC ordination level” or use the number alone for CRUD. `BaseController::resolveDashboardScope()` independently chooses a landing page by group (`app/Controllers/BaseController.php:950-1093`).
2. **Most appointment infrastructure already exists.** `leadership_position` has `user_id`, `person_id` (added by a later migration), `scope_type`, `parish_id`/`diocese_id`/`province_id`, `role_code`, `title`, `role_category`, `clergy_order`, start/end dates, `active`, and `appointed_by_user_id` (`app/Models/LeadershipPositionModel.php`; `app/Database/Migrations/2026-08-01-180558_AlignLeadershipPositionToPersonId.php`). Reuse it for offices; add `deanery` to its scope type and a `deanery_id` through migration. `report_clergy` is a report snapshot, not a canonical clergy registry. The legacy `user_scope_assignment` table was deliberately dropped by `2026-07-28-221941_DropUserScopeAssignmentTable.php`; do **not** restore it as a second source of truth without a separate design decision.
3. **The attachment's rank check is incorrect/redundant.** Given higher number = broader tier, `assignment.scope.rank <= target.rank` would reject a diocese (2) acting on its parish (1). Hierarchical authorization must compare **entity ancestry/identity**, not just numbers: a diocesan grant for Diocese A can apply to a parish *in A*, but not Diocese B or Province IX. A deanery grant applies only to member parishes of **that** deanery and only for explicitly allowed actions. It must still have the specific permission for the action. Account-target mutations additionally pass the existing 4–0 guard; deanery does not add a rank.

### Current gaps the plan must close

- Shield `clergy` is presently a broad, globally effective access group: it includes messaging, visitor edits, donations/financial viewing, and `governance.report.finalize_clergy` (`app/Config/AuthGroups.php:420-457`). It does **not** distinguish a deacon from a rector, nor one parish from another. `dioceseadmin` has `governance.diocese.approve` irrespective of ecclesial office (`:350-375`). `systemadmin` has both finalization and diocesan approval (`:304-328`). These are **current app permissions**, not claims of canonically vested office.
- `LeadershipPositionService::eligibilityFor()` treats a submitted `role_category=clergy` as sufficient to skip lay good-standing checks; it does not verify ordination (`app/Services/LeadershipPositionService.php:121-130,299-345`). Both appointment pathways accept a submitted `clergy_order` and free-text `role_code` (`Clergy/Controllers/People.php:720-825`; `MemberPortal/Controllers/LeadershipPositions.php:109-204`). An existing test even uses `role_category=clergy` for a **Parish Warden** (`tests/database/LeadershipPositionsScopeIntegrationTest.php:42-75`). Treat existing rows as unverified, not as proof of orders.
- Both appointment pathways gate most writes with `member.edit` rather than the already-defined `leadership_position.manage` (`Clergy/Controllers/People.php:720-749,830-862`; `MemberPortal/Controllers/LeadershipPositions.php:109-141,206-249`). `LeadershipPositionService::canManage()` checks matching stored scope IDs, but not whether the actor is *entitled to manage that tier* (`app/Services/LeadershipPositionService.php:97-119`). Fix checks centrally in both flows.
- `PermissionRoleSyncSeeder` **adds** per-user grants from the matrix but does not remove obsolete grants (`app/Database/Seeds/PermissionRoleSyncSeeder.php:20-56`). This conflicts with the current prose claiming strict removal in `docs/ROLE_MENU_PERMISSION_POLICY.md:43-48`. Revocation needs an explicit, provenance-aware migration and ongoing synchronization; removing a group alone may leave stale `auth_permissions_users` grants.
- Reporting needs a schema/workflow reconciliation before adding gates: `app/Models/ParishReportModel.php:14-23,64-92` describes submitted/approved/published records, while `app/Database/canonical_schema_tables.sql` defines a different, year-keyed `parish_report` shape with `report_status`/`finalized_at`; there is also `parish_financial_summary` with finalization fields. The discovered permission names are **not evidence of a finished canonical approval endpoint**. First determine the actual migrated schema and distinguish parish report, financial certification, and diocesan approval.

## 2. Episcopal domain model: four independent axes

| Axis                                | Meaning                                                                                                                                 | Recommended record                                                                                                                               | Grants app access by itself?                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ordained order**                  | `deacon`, `priest`, `bishop`; or no verified order. A deacon may be vocational or transitional.                                         | Person-linked verified ordination history/status, with effective dates, verifying authority, and optional canonical-residence/licensing records. | **No.** Qualifies someone for compatible offices and certain functions only.                                                             |
| **Ecclesial office / appointment**  | Rector, priest-in-charge, vicar, cathedral dean, regional/deanery dean, diocesan bishop, suffragan bishop, curate, parish deacon, etc.  | Controlled `role_code` on a time-bounded `leadership_position` row with one concrete parish/deanery/diocese/province scope.                      | **Not automatically.** An approved, active office may confer a *narrow* policy-defined app capability.                                   |
| **Administrative tier**             | `systemadmin=4`, `provinceadmin=3`, `dioceseadmin=2`, `parishadmin=1`, everyone else `=0`.                                              | Existing Shield groups; highest tier for account-hierarchy protections and current landing behavior.                                             | Only in combination with a permitted operation and verified entity scope. A `clergy` group stays tier **0**.                             |
| **App capability/delegation**       | Ability to see a directory, approve a report, maintain a roster, manage finances, administer users.                                     | Shield permission plus effective assignment/grant, exact entity scope, dates, and audit provenance.                                              | **Yes, only after all scope/status/eligibility checks pass.** (A permission does not carry its own scope; scope is added by assignment.) |

Avoid a mutable `is_clergy` boolean as a second truth: derive it from verified order/status, allowing historic ordinations while marking inhibition, retirement, transfer, and licensing separately. Tie ordination to **person**, not one login account. Canonical residence or licensure in a diocese is a relationship, **not** a permission grant or a pastoral office. Continue to support multiple concurrent offices and historic tenures; only offices specifically designated as unique (for example one current ordinary per diocese) need uniqueness enforcement. An individual may have no portal account and still belong in a clergy registry; requiring a portal identity is appropriate only when granting application access, not when recording ordination/appointment.

| ---------------------------------------------------------| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |

A person without a portal account still receives no app access. |
| Bishop coadjutor; suffragan; assisting bishop            | Bishop                                                             | One diocese or dated delegated remit                                            | No ordinary's approval or admin powers by title; grant exactly delegated capabilities and duration. Retired bishop: none unless actively licensed/appointed. |
| Rector                                                   | Priest                                                             | One parish congregation                                                         | Parish pastoral leadership and clergy-side parochial-report finalization after required lay governance steps; no default financial CRUD, account admin, or diocesan approval. Election/approval details belong to the appointment record.                                                                                                                     |
| Priest-in-charge; interim rector; vicar                  | Priest                                                             | One congregation/mission represented as a parish record                         | Parish pastoral/report finalization while the appointment is active and designated as the congregation's priest-in-charge. Distinguish appointment mode and tenure from settled rector.                                                                                                                                |
| Cathedral dean                                           | Priest                                                             | A cathedral congregation represented by a specifically typed parish record      | Cathedral/parish pastoral/report capability as the priest-in-charge; **not** diocesan admin.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

May simultaneously hold a separate parish office.                                                 |

A canon may be ordained **or lay**; do not infer order from “canon.”                                                                                                                                                  |
| Parish deacon; diocesan archdeacon                       | Deacon for parish-deacon; archdeacon eligibility per local canons  | Named parish/diocese or delegated territory                                     | Appropriate liturgical/pastoral/diaconal service; no priest-only rector/dean office. Administrative delegations, if any, are separate.                                                                                                                                                                               |

The [Episcopal Church definition of rector](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/glossary/rector/) requires a priest elected by the vestry and approved by the bishop; the [Diocese of Texas transition glossary](https://www.epicenter.org/for-leaders/transition-ministry/transition-glossary-terms/) distinguishes rector, priest-in-charge, interim rector, and vicar. The Church distinguishes the [ordinary from suffragan/assistant/coadjutor bishops](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/glossary/ordinary-of-a-diocese/) and [vocational from transitional deacons](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/glossary/deacon/). Its [style guide](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/the-undercroft-2/communications-and-branding-resources/writing-style-guide/titles/) distinguishes cathedral dean from a bishop, and ordained from lay canons. These are **modeling guides**; approve jurisdiction-specific canon-law and local reporting delegations with the diocese before activating capabilities. The [Episcopal Church identifies the Dominican Republic diocese within Province IX](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/source/public-affairs/mcqueen-expands-role-as-episcopal-church-partnership-officer-for-latin-america-and-the-caribbean/); do not equate a diocesan bishop with a provincial or TEC-wide system administrator.

## 3. Permission and group design

**Keep** the existing admin groups and 4–0 guard for their present account-management purpose. Do **not** add Shield groups for `bishop`, `priest`, `deacon`, `rector`, `dean`, or `priest_in_charge`: a global group cannot carry a parish/diocese ID or expiry, and “bishop” is an order while “rector” is an office. Keep the existing `clergy` group temporarily as a **limited portal-access compatibility group**, not canonical status; narrow its blanket grants after usage audit and gradually move sensitive actions to scoped office capabilities. Never auto-add `dioceseadmin` for a bishop; if a bishop also administers the app, issue a separately approved scoped admin delegation with an expiry/revocation path. Conversely, a lay diocesan administrator may retain technical data/admin privileges without being labeled bishop or gaining sacramental/canonical eligibility.

Proposed permission families (names finalized alongside existing routes):

- `clergy.directory.view`, `clergy.ordination.verify`, `clergy.appointment.manage`: distinct roster, evidence verification, and appointment administration. Appointees cannot verify their own orders or appoint themselves; require a higher-scope authorized reviewer and audit.
- `pastoral.parish.view` / `pastoral.parish.manage`: limited in-scope pastoral operations, with sensitive notes and contact data separately governed. A deacon or curate need not receive all rector capabilities.
- `deanery.view` / `deanery.coordinate`: narrowly limited in-deanery roster and pastoral coordination; `deanery.manage` for explicitly authorized diocesan officers who define deaneries and their parish membership. A regional dean's office does not automatically receive the manage capability or parish financial/member-edit permissions.
- Keep `governance.report.finalize_clergy` but require a **currently authorized priest-in-charge office** and that parish's report, not merely `inGroup('clergy')`; keep `governance.diocese.approve` but require its diocesan ordinary's active appointment or an explicitly recorded diocesan delegation, in that diocese. A technical `systemadmin` correction is separately audited and **must not impersonate an ecclesial signature**.
- Retain `member.edit`, finance, group/staff administration, and account reset as **independent** operations. No pastoral title implicitly yields financial or platform-admin rights. Split or replace existing broadly named grants only after enumerating each route and migration impact.

Illustrative **deny-by-default** check for an ecclesial operation:

```text
canEcclesialAction(actor, action, target):
  require authenticated, active, onboarded actor
  require route-level Shield permission for action
  require target's actual entity ID and ancestor IDs from database
  require currently effective, approved office/delegation for actor.person_id
  require role-policy allows action AND office.scope contains target's scope
  require office order matches independently verified current ordination/status
  require no suspension/inhibition/expired license or other blocking status
  if target is a user being mutated: ALSO apply AdminHierarchyGuard
  otherwise deny (403, or 404 for an out-of-scope private record)
```

**Crucial:** Shield permission is global once granted, so never treat `$user->can('...')` alone, a group membership, a stored profile parish, or the 4–0 rank as proof of jurisdiction. Scoped rights may apply down the tree **only where the capability policy explicitly allows it**. Permission filters protect the route; controller/service checks protect the *specific resource*. Do not change `BaseController::isElevatedAdmin()`, `canBypassPendingReviewGate()`, or `resolveDashboardScope()` to promote clergy automatically; offer a separately chosen diocesan landing path only where office-specific portal workflows justify it.

## 5. Resolved first-release policy decisions

These are **recommended product decisions for implementation**, not a finding that local diocesan canons or the user have already approved every ecclesial delegation. Keep policy data explicit so diocesan authorities can adjust a local exception without rewriting authorization architecture.

### 5.1 Geographic scope

**Release 1 supports clergy workflows for any diocese in The Episcopal Church that is configured in ekklesiaRD**, including its parishes, cathedrals, missions, and deaneries. Retain the existing System → Province → Diocese → Parish administrative group/rank hierarchy: **deanery is an ecclesial scope under a diocese, not a fifth admin tier**. For resource containment, a parish may be directly under its diocese or also belong to one active deanery (`diocese → deanery → parish`); that membership never changes the parish's existing `diocese_id`. Do not hardcode the Dominican Republic, Province IX, or a single diocese into queries, seeders, eligibility, approval, or UI. Onboard each diocese with its correct province relationship and congregation records; clergy verification, appointments, and approvals are bounded to the **specific configured diocese and its actual descendant congregations**. A deanery office is bounded to its own deanery's affiliated congregations and narrower coordination capabilities; it does not confer a diocesan-wide role. A diocesan office or admin grant for Diocese A never applies to Diocese B, even if both belong to the same province. No assignment, ordination, dashboard choice, or shared province silently grants province-wide or TEC-wide authority; those require separate, explicit, scoped app grants and policy. An unconfigured diocese is not an implicit tenancy/access grant. Existing province-wide reporting/admin features retain their own authorization rules and are not expanded by clergy appointments. `systemadmin=4` means platform administration, **not** presiding-bishop office; `provinceadmin=3` means an app administrator, **not** an ordained provincial superior. The [Dominican Republic's Province IX affiliation](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/source/public-affairs/mcqueen-expands-role-as-episcopal-church-partnership-officer-for-latin-america-and-the-caribbean/) is one example of an ecclesial relationship, **not** an authorization rule or launch restriction.

### 5.2 Bishop appointment and digital authority

An independently verified **bishop order** qualifies a person for an approved, dated `diocesan_bishop` **office** attached to one diocese. Approval of that office should provision only the diocesan pastoral/governance capabilities needed for the workflow—principally **view diocesan reports and ecclesially approve reports in that diocese**—to a linked, active portal account. Eligibility checks still require the active office and exact diocese on every action; revoke effective permissions when the appointment ends, is suspended, or the account is disabled. Do not assign `dioceseadmin`, `provinceadmin`, `systemadmin`, financial CRUD, parish user management, or cross-diocese authority automatically. Provide a separate audited, scoped and time-bounded digital-admin delegation if the same person also does technical administration.

A suffragan, coadjutor, assisting, or retired bishop **does not inherit the ordinary's app approval**. Grant a specific diocesan reporting delegation with issuer, scope, term, and revocation if local policy permits; a coadjutor's succession to ordinary requires an explicit office transition. Only one effective ordinary is permitted per diocese at a time (validate overlap transactionally), but multiple assisting bishops/other offices may exist. The [Episcopal Church's ordinary definition](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/glossary/ordinary-of-a-diocese/) motivates this distinction.

### 5.3 Parish report workflow and sign-off

**First reconcile the report schema and choose a single canonical report workflow.** Existing `governance.*` permission names and `ParishReportModel` methods do not prove a complete endpoint, and the model's fields differ from the canonical-schema snapshot. The approval policy below applies to the canonical parish/annual report **when that workflow is implemented**; do not mistake financial-summary finalization fields for ecclesial parish-report signatures.

| Step                                              | Eligible actor                                                                                                                                  | Scope and restriction                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prepare/submit                                    | Authorized parish reporter/staff                                                                                                                | Report for the actor's parish only; does not imply ecclesial sign-off.                                                                                          |
| Certify governance record                         | Vestry secretary/clerk in a parish with a vestry                                                                                                | Appropriate parish appointment/permission; no clergy-order inference. For a mission without that office, require a separately configured bishop-designated local governance signer, not an automatic bypass. |
| Vestry/local governance approval                  | Warden/vestry-designated actor for an established parish; bishop-designated mission approver otherwise                                          | Appropriate congregation-scoped governance grant; separate from priestly finalization.                                                                        |
| **Clergy finalization**                           | Current rector, priest-in-charge, interim rector, vicar, or cathedral dean **who is the designated priest-in-charge of that congregation**      | Verified priest order and effective parish appointment; that parish's report only. A deacon, curate, bishop with no such parish appointment, or generic `clergy` group does not qualify. In a vacancy, an explicitly named, licensed priest delegate with a dated parish grant may act. |
| **Diocesan ecclesial approval**                   | Current diocesan ordinary or an explicitly bishop-delegated, dated diocesan approver permitted under local canons                               | Report's actual diocese only; store **who acted, office/delegation, diocese, timestamp, and whether action was delegated**. A technical `dioceseadmin` group alone is insufficient. |

Keep the stages separate; reject self-certification across lay governance, clergy finalization, and diocesan approval even if one account holds multiple groups. After approval, lock the signed version or create a new revision rather than silently replacing signed content. A `systemadmin` may perform a distinct, reason-coded **technical correction** if operationally necessary, but must not be recorded as the bishop's or rector's ecclesial approval. Confirm the precise clerk/warden/delegation requirements against **each configured diocese's** local policy before enabling its production signatures; these defaults are implementation guardrails, not a statement of universal canon law. The [Episcopal rector definition](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/glossary/rector/) and [transition glossary](https://www.epicenter.org/for-leaders/transition-ministry/transition-glossary-terms/) support the distinct offices.

### 5.4 Dean offices and congregation type

**Implement both cathedral dean and regional/deanery dean in release 1; they are different offices.** Cathedral dean is a **priest** appointed to lead a particular cathedral congregation; store `cathedral_dean` as an office on a `parish`-scoped `leadership_position`, with an explicit cathedral/mission/parish **congregation type** on the existing parish entity if not already available. Do not infer cathedral status from a name or equate the dean to `dioceseadmin`. The cathedral dean may finalize **that cathedral's** report only if recorded as its current priest-in-charge. A cathedral chapter role is a separate, scoped appointment if required.

Create a `deanery` entity owned by exactly one diocese and explicit, effective-dated membership for its constituent parishes. For a deanery's office, extend `leadership_position.scope_type` and its validators/schema to accept `deanery` with an exclusive `deanery_id`; store `regional_dean` or `deanery_dean` as a controlled office code, **not** a Shield group. The diocesan bishop (or an authorized appointee acting for the bishop under the diocese's policy) approves the effective, dated appointment. Require a verified **priest** by default; make a diocese-specific variation possible only after documented local policy review. During the office term, allow only necessary deanery-wide pastoral coordination and limited directory/communications access for member congregations. No automatic parish CRUD, finance, account administration, clergy report finalization, diocesan approval, or `dioceseadmin`. A regional dean who is also a rector needs a **separate parish-scoped rector appointment** for rector actions. Regional/deanery leaders must never be routed to all parishes simply because `deanery.diocese_id` matches; verify **current** membership of the target parish in that precise deanery for current access. Historical membership is for historical attribution, not an access grant.

This follows the [Diocese of Massachusetts description of deaneries as groups of congregations with bishop-appointed priest deans for pastoral care/communication](https://www.diomass.org/about-us/deaneries-and-regions) and the [Diocese of Virginia's separately appointed regional deans](https://episcopalvirginia.org/who-we-are/governance/regions-deans-presidents/). The [Episcopal Church style guide](https://www.episcopalchurch.org/the-undercroft-2/communications-and-branding-resources/writing-style-guide/titles/) identifies the cathedral dean as a distinct cathedral office; dioceses may use different regional names and local appointment practices.

**Registry identity rule:** Record verified orders and historical offices for people **without** portal accounts; grant no Shield/application permissions until an active user identity is linked, onboarding is complete, and a scoped office/delegation is approved. This resolves the fifth question from the earlier draft without limiting the clergy directory to users who can sign in.
