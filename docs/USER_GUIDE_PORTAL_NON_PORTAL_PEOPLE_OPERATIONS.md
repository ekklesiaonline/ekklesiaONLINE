# User Guide: Operating Users, Members, Guests, and Children (Portal and Non-Portal)

## Audience

This guide is for church admins, pastors, and authorized staff who manage people records in ekklesiaRD.

## What This Guide Covers

- How to manage members with portal access
- How to manage non-portal (non-access) members
- How to register and follow up guests
- How to add/manage children in families
- How to keep data scope-safe and consistent

## Quick Terms

- Portal member: Can log in (has enabled account access).
- Non-portal member: Exists in records but cannot log in.
- Guest: Visitor record (not yet a member account).
- Child/children: Family-linked member records (may be portal or non-portal).

## Before You Start

1. Confirm you are in the correct church context.
2. Confirm your role has people/family management permissions.
3. Use church-scoped pages only for your assigned church unless you are super admin.

## 1. Managing Members

## A. Create a Non-Portal Member (No Login Access)

Use this when the person should be tracked but should not log in yet.

1. Open Families and go to the target family.
2. Choose Add Member (Non-Portal).
3. Enter person details (name, relationship, optional birth date).
4. Submit.

Expected result:

- Member record is created and linked to family.
- Member is affiliated with the church.
- Portal access remains disabled.

Use cases:

- Children not ready for login
- Elderly members who do not use the portal
- Members pending digital onboarding

## B. Enable Portal Access for an Existing Non-Portal Member

Use this when a member now needs login access.

1. Open family detail and locate the member.
2. Click Enable Portal.
3. Enter valid email + temporary password.
4. Submit.

Expected result:

- Login identity is created.
- Member is assigned portal member access.
- Access state becomes enabled.

Important:

- Email must be unique.
- Member must belong to that family and church scope.

## C. Managing Existing Portal Members

1. Search member in People/Families.
2. Update profile details as needed.
3. Keep church affiliation and membership status accurate.
4. If portal issues occur, verify identity email and group assignment.

## 2. Managing Guests

Use guest flow for first-time or non-member visitors.

1. Open Guests module.
2. Add guest details (name, contact, visit date, service attended).
3. Save guest.
4. Create/assign follow-up task.
5. Mark follow-up outcome.

Do not create a portal user for guests unless they are being converted to members.

## A. Convert Guest to Member (When Needed)

1. Confirm guest has requested/accepted membership onboarding.
2. Create member using non-portal or portal flow depending on access need.
3. If family-linked, add to the correct family.
4. Update guest follow-up record to reflect conversion.

## 3. Managing Children (Childs/Children)

Use family-based workflows for children.

1. Open the family record.
2. Add child as a family member.
3. Set relationship (Child/Son/Daughter as your policy uses).
4. Usually start as non-portal unless login is required.
5. Enable portal later when appropriate.

Best practice:

- Keep child records linked to family to preserve household reporting.
- Avoid creating duplicate child entries in multiple families.

## 4. Family Operations

## A. Create Family

1. Go to Families.
2. Create new family with family name.
3. Optionally assign head of household.
4. Save.

## B. Add/Remove Family Members

1. Open family details.
2. Add member or remove relation.
3. Confirm scope and relationship are correct.

Important:

- Members should only be added if affiliated with the same church.
- Remove operations only detach from family; they do not necessarily delete user/member records.

## 5. Portal vs Non-Portal Decision Guide

Choose Non-Portal when:

- Person does not need self-service access now.
- Record is administrative only.

Choose Portal when:

- Person needs profile access, notifications, or portal workflows.
- Person can manage credentials securely.

## 6. Common Errors and What to Do

- Out of scope: You are trying to manage a record from another church. Switch to correct church context.
- User not found: Record ID is invalid or deleted. Refresh and search again.
- Email already in use: Use another email or resolve duplicate identity first.
- Validation failed: Check required fields and format.
- Portal already enabled: No further action needed unless resetting credentials.

## 7. Data Quality Checklist

After create/update operations, verify:

1. Correct church scope
2. Correct family linkage
3. Correct membership status
4. Correct portal status (none/enabled)
5. No duplicate member/family links

## 8. Security and Privacy Practices

- Never share temporary passwords in open channels.
- Avoid exporting personal data unless authorized.
- Do not log sensitive credentials.
- Use role-appropriate access only.

## 9. Recommended Admin Routine (Weekly)

1. Review newly added guests and follow-up completion.
2. Review non-portal members pending portal activation.
3. Review family records for duplicates/inconsistencies.
4. Review membership status updates (active/inactive/visitor/pending).

## 10. Escalation

Escalate to technical/admin support when:

- Scope/permission behavior seems incorrect.
- Portal enablement fails repeatedly with valid inputs.
- Duplicate identity or data inconsistency cannot be resolved in UI.
- Audit trail shows unexpected changes.
