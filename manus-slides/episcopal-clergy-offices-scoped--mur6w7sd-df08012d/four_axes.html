Four independent axes prevent role inflation.

A title describes a person or office; it does not establish digital jurisdiction.

01 / PERSON

Ordained order

Verified deacon, priest, bishop—or no order. Canonical fact; no app access by itself.

02 / APPOINTMENT

Ecclesial office

Rector, ordinary, cathedral dean, regional dean. One approved, dated scope per office.

03 / PLATFORM

Admin priority

Shield system 4 · province 3 · diocese 2 · parish 1 · other 0. Protects account mutations.

04 / ACTION

Digital permission

Operation + exact entity ID + active office/delegation. Revocable and auditable.
