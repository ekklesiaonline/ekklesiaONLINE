Similar clerical titles have different jurisdictions.

Approved office and verified order must both match the requested action.

OfficeOrderScopeLimited app capability
Diocesan ordinaryBishopOne dioceseDiocesan pastoral review and canonical report approval
Rector / priest-in-chargePriestOne congregationParish pastoral work and clergy report finalization
Cathedral deanPriestOne cathedral congregationCathedral pastoral work; finalizes only as priest-in-charge
Regional deanPriest by defaultOne defined deaneryLimited regional coordination; no parish CRUD
Deacon / curateDeacon / priestAssigned ministryTask-specific ministry; no automatic sign-off
No office automatically confers finance, app-account management, province, or TEC-wide rights.
