Geographic & Operational Boundaries

Province Boundary
ProvinceScopeFilter

Validates that dioceses and parishes in GET/POST parameters belong to the actor's resolved province.

Diocese Boundary
DiocesanScopeFilter

Restores compatibility for legacy route declarations while sharing DioceseScopeFilter enforcement logic.

Parish Boundary
ParishScopeFilter

Enforces a valid selected or profile parish context for operator workflows; bypassed by higher-level admins.

Operational Boundary
Functional Gates

Blocks diocesan admins from parish-operator modules and restricts province/diocese admins from finance workflows.
