# Ordinary and episcopal authorization

This document covers diocesan-bishop (ordinary) authority: ordination verification, canonical standing, office approval, delegation and bootstrap. For deanery creation, membership and the regional/deanery dean scope, see the companion [deanery authorization guide](DEANERY_AUTHORIZATION.md).

**Status:** clergy/deanery schema **installed on live MySQL** in migration batch 94; ecclesial office authorization is **not yet provisioned**. The [post-migration audit](../outputs/post-migration-role-audit-2026-10-02.md) confirmed all expected tables/columns, one pending legacy candidate and no verified orders, approved offices or managed grants. Preserve the earlier [pre-migration snapshot](../outputs/live-migration-role-audit-2026-10-02.md) for historical comparison. MySQL-clone testing and backups remain essential for future forward migrations.

## Boundaries

Technical admin rank is unchanged: `systemadmin=4`, `provinceadmin=3`, `dioceseadmin=2`, `parishadmin=1`, all other groups `0`. These are account-target administration priorities, **not** ecclesial orders. A bishop is not an automatic diocesan app admin; a cathedral dean is not a diocesan admin; a regional dean has only their recorded deanery's current member congregations. No grant expands to Province IX, another province, or all of The Episcopal Church by implication. See [the implementation plan](../outputs/clergy-ordination-office-authorization-plan.md) and the [canonical group policy](ROLE_MENU_PERMISSION_POLICY.md).

The migration adds `parish.congregation_type` (`parish` | `cathedral` | `mission`), `leadership_position.deanery_id`, `pastoral_in_charge`, `approved_by_user_id`, `approved_at`, `approval_evidence_reference`, `revoked_at`, and report-stage columns. A cathedral dean requires both an explicit cathedral classification and a reviewed `pastoral_in_charge=1` to finalize that cathedral's report. A regional dean never finalizes on the strength of a deanery appointment alone. The generated migration is additive and performs preflight checks **before MySQL DDL**; rollback refuses to discard reviewed clergy or signed-report history.

The `clergy` Shield group remains a baseline operational label, not proof of ordination or a fourth admin tier. The two mission groups provide coarse keys only: the checked appointment, mission, review and identity still govern signing. A diocesan app admin may configure deanery membership in their assigned diocese; this does not let them verify orders or sign as bishop.

## Canon to the ordinary

`canon_to_ordinary` is registered in `LeadershipPositionService::CLERGY_OFFICES` as a **diocese-scoped, priest-order** office (`['diocese', 'priest']`). It models the strict "canon proposes, ordinary approves" workflow:

- The canon is a **title-only office**, exactly like `suffragan_bishop`/`coadjutor_bishop` carry no keys by office alone — but stricter: `canon_to_ordinary` is deliberately **not** in `ClergyReviewService::OFFICE_PERMISSIONS` and **not** in `ClergyAuthorizationService::POLICY`. `approveOffice()` rejects any `role_code` outside `OFFICE_PERMISSIONS`, so a canon position can never be approved, produces zero `clergy_access_grant` rows, and the holder can never exercise `clergy.appointment.manage`, `clergy.ordination.verify` or `governance.diocese.approve`.
- The canon may **stage** offices only. Staging via `POST /leadership/store` / `LeadershipPositionService::create()` still requires the *actor* to hold the Shield coarse key `leadership_position.manage` with `dioceseadmin` scope over the position's diocese (`LeadershipPositionService::canManage()`), plus the controller's ecclesial check (`lacksEcclesialAuthority()`): `pastoral.parish.manage` over the parish for parish offices, `clergy.appointment.manage` over the admin's assigned diocese for deanery/diocese offices. The ecclesial title alone grants no staging rights — in practice the staging actor is the ordinary themselves or a diocesan app admin holding `clergy.appointment.manage` (e.g. via ordinary delegation), who stages the `canon_to_ordinary` position and then the subordinate offices the canon proposes.
- Every staged office (rector, priest_in_charge, cathedral_dean, deacons, regional_dean, etc.) is created **unapproved**; the diocesan bishop must personally call `ClergyReviewService::approveOffice()` (`POST /diocese/clergy-review/office/{positionId}/approve`) for each one. `canReview()` requires the reviewer to hold `clergy.appointment.manage` over that diocese and to be a distinct person from the appointee — the canon can satisfy neither.

## Ordinary bootstrap

The first ordinary cannot digitally self-verify or self-approve. `ClergyReviewService::installOrdinary()` is the audited system-admin bypass that establishes a diocese's first verified bishop: it records the ordination, standing, diocesan-bishop office and the ordinary's `clergy_access_grant` rows in one transaction. It is the only system-admin bypass in clergy review alongside `endOrdinaryOffice()`. No account is auto-promoted from a title, legacy group or candidate.

## Office approval

`ClergyReviewService::approveOffice()` approves a staged `leadership_position` only when the role code exists in `OFFICE_PERMISSIONS`, the position validates (`validPositionPayload`, active, unapproved, current term, portal access), and the reviewer passes `canReview()` — `clergy.appointment.manage` over the containing diocese and a different person than the appointee. Approval inserts one `clergy_access_grant` row per mapped permission with `grant_type=office` and `source_position_id` provenance. `revokeOffice()` ends the office and removes only its managed grants.

## Delegation

`issueDelegation()` lets the current ordinary confer a listed governance/review key on a named user for the exact diocese, dated and revocable, with documentary evidence. `issueParishVacancyDelegation()` is the parish-scoped form: a verified licensed priest, an exact vacant parish, a time limit; it stops conferring access once an incumbent assumes charge. `revokeDelegation()` ends a delegation and removes only its managed grants.

## Ending the office

`endOrdinaryOffice()` (system-admin bypass) or `revokeOffice()` ends a diocesan bishop's office. Ending or revoking it also revokes the delegations that bishop issued in that diocese, including parish-scoped delegations — see `lastRevokedDelegationIds()`. This does not revoke offices the bishop approved or ordinations they verified.

## Canonical status and standing

Suspension and inhibition apply to the person in every diocese until their `effective_to` passes; transfer and retirement remain local to the recorded diocese. `liftStanding()` closes a suspension/inhibition window early by writing `effective_to` plus lift evidence, and is guarded against double-lifting and invalid reviewers. `recordStanding()` documents a new standing row; `activePositiveStandingCount()` reports current positive standing.

Every action re-evaluates order, date, identity, scope and status. Enabling the diocesan flag alone does not appoint signers; assigning a technical admin group alone does not approve an office. If editable storage changes after submission, signatures stop until a new revision archives the previous version. Report consumers must use `ParishReportModel::signedSnapshot()` for an approved version, never the mutable storage row.

## Controlled rollout

1. **Inspect the installed schema and preserve a backup.** All three formerly pending migrations were recorded in batch 94; the [post-migration read-only audit](../outputs/post-migration-role-audit-2026-10-02.md) confirms nine tables, 17 columns and one pending candidate. Do not re-run applied migration classes or execute the old draft in `outputs/`.
2. For future FK hardening or other forward migrations, rehearse against a **MySQL clone** and verify deletion/rollback behavior — see the [deanery guide](DEANERY_AUTHORIZATION.md#known-schema-gap) for the `leadership_position.deanery_id` note.
3. **Review the 17 untracked legacy direct signing grants before any permission sync or cleanup.** `permission_role_sync_managed` contains no rows and cannot assign origin to historic direct grants. Do not mass-delete them or assume `php spark db:seed PermissionRoleSyncSeeder` will remove them. This agent did not run that seeder.
4. For **each** diocese, independently confirm person identity, ordination documents, standing, current ordinary and appointment. **Bootstrap gap:** the first ordinary cannot digitally self-verify or self-approve; an authorized, audited two-person diocesan record review/controlled import (`installOrdinary()`) must establish the first verified bishop and approved office. No account is auto-promoted from a title, legacy group or candidate. Do not bypass `pending_review` onboarding.
5. Review legacy candidates; appoint the exact deanery/parish offices and lay signers; document cathedral type and explicit pastoral charge. Have the verified ordinary enable reporting policy with the local evidence reference only after confirming the diocese's vestry/mission signatory policy.
6. Run `php spark clergy:reconcile-grants` to inspect expired/revoked **managed** grant sources. After backup/review, `php spark clergy:reconcile-grants --apply` removes only provenance-linked stale direct permissions. Apply mode is scheduled via the cron wrapper `build/clergy_reconcile_grants_cron.sh` (recommended hourly, or at least daily; it appends to `writable/logs/clergy_reconcile_grants_cron.log`), so expired or revoked office and delegation grants stop conferring their synced Shield permissions automatically. Continue to revoke explicitly on personnel changes rather than waiting for the next run.
7. Re-run `vendor/bin/phpunit --no-coverage --do-not-cache-result` (the 128 MB CLI default needs result caching disabled for the full suite), permission-role sync regression tests, and a staged end-to-end sign-off for two unrelated dioceses (including dioceses in the same and different provinces). Validate profile onboarding gates and negative cross-scope/expired-role cases before enabling each diocese.

**The user applied the migrations; this agent only verified their effects read-only.** The staging migration created one pending candidate but no verified order or automatic permission. This agent performed no live role/permission edit, data backfill, seeding, or policy enablement. No Git commit, push, or deployment was created by this agent.
