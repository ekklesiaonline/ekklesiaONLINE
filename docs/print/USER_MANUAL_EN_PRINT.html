1. Purpose

EkklesiaRD is a multi-level Parish Management System for province oversight, diocesan administration, and parish-level operations. Access is controlled by role and permission scope.

2. Access and Login

  1. Open the system URL provided by your administrator.
  2. Sign in with your email and password.
  3. If your account is pending review, wait for diocesan approval.
  4. After approval, you are redirected to your role dashboard.

3. Role-Based Dashboards

Province Administrator

  • Network-wide diocesan summaries
  • Scope and period filters
  • Create dioceses and assign diocesan admins

Diocesan Administrator

  • Manage parishes under one diocese
  • Moderate new registrations
  • Open finance and system-overview dashboards

Parish-Level Roles

  • Daily ministry and administration operations
  • Membership, finance, attendance, and follow-up visibility

4. Main Navigation Menu

Common menu entries include Dashboard, Members/Staff, Families, Services, Attendance, Guests, Guest Conversions, Leadership, Good Standing, Follow-ups, Finance, Volunteers, Events, Worship Planning, Reports, Parishes, Profile, and Logout.

5. Core Workflows

5.1 Member Management

  1. Open Members.
  2. Search by name/email/phone.
  3. Open the record.
  4. Update data and save.

5.2 Family Management

  1. Open Families.
  2. Create household and assign relationships.
  3. Save and verify.

5.3 Services and Attendance

  1. Create service.
  2. Open attendance page.
  3. Mark attendees and communicants.
  4. Review attendance reports.

5.4 Guests and Conversion

  1. Register guest.
  2. Create follow-up task.
  3. Complete follow-up.
  4. Convert guest if appropriate.

5.5 Leadership and Good Standing

  1. Maintain leadership assignments.
  2. Open Good Standing page.
  3. Run refresh and validate outcomes.

5.6 Finance Operations

Use Donations, Expenses, Budgets, and Annual Pledges to maintain financial records and review net position.

5.7 Volunteers and Events

Manage teams, assignments, schedules, event registrations, and check-ins.

6. Diocesan Registration Moderation Workflow

  1. Open Diocesan Dashboard.
  2. Review pending registrations.
  3. Select role and parish scope.
  4. Approve, Dismiss, Ban, or Delete.

7. Province Oversight Workflow

  1. Open Province Dashboard.
  2. Choose diocese scope and period.
  3. Review attendance and membership summaries.
  4. Create dioceses or assign diocesan admins.

8. Finance Dashboard Workflow (Diocesan)

  1. Open Diocesan Finance Dashboard.
  2. Toggle parish scope: all or single parish.
  3. Apply period filter.
  4. Review income, expenses, net, and trend charts.

9. Reports and Data Quality

Use Reports for operational and financial outputs. Use Consistency Report to detect and export integrity issues.

10. Profile and Account

Users maintain profile details and complete required fields for full access.

11. Permissions and Visibility Rules

  • Menus and actions are permission-based.
  • Parish users are scoped to their parish.
  • Diocesan users are scoped to their diocese.
  • Province users are scoped to provincial summaries.

12. Troubleshooting

  • Missing menu item: verify role and permissions.
  • Unexpected redirect: verify parish/diocese scope.
  • Empty dashboard: verify date filters and data entry.
  • Pending registration: request diocesan review.

13. Recommended Operating Routine

Daily Dashboard checks, follow-ups, attendance, finance entries.

Weekly Conversion and good-standing review; volunteer and event planning.

Monthly Financial reconciliation, budget and pledge review, report export.

14. Screenshot Guide

No screenshots are bundled in the repository. Capture role-specific dashboard and module views and store under docs/images.

15. Quick Start by Role

Province Admin: province summary and governance actions.

Diocesan Admin: registration moderation and diocesan tools.

Parish Admin: end-to-end local operations.

Pastor: members, attendance, follow-up, worship.

Treasurer: donations, expenses, budgets, pledges, reports.

Volunteer Coordinator: teams, assignments, schedules.

Member: profile and permitted self-service actions.
